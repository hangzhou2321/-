# Third-party boundaries

ManiSoft-RL does not bundle the upstream ManiSoft simulator, ManiSoft datasets,
FlightGear aircraft packages, or trained checkpoints.

- ManiSoft is an external runtime distributed by its maintainers under the MIT
  License: https://github.com/buaa-colalab/ManiSoft
- Optional FlightGear visual assets are obtained separately and remain subject
  to the license shipped with each asset package.
- Users are responsible for reviewing and complying with the licenses of all
  external runtimes, datasets, models, and assets they choose to install.

Names and source references in code or documentation identify compatibility or
geometry provenance; they do not bundle or relicense third-party content.
